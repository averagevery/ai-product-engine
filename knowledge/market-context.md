# Mulo AI: Market Context & Segments

## Customer Segments
* **Primary Segment**: UK adults aged 22–40 making first major financial decisions without confidence.
* **Core Insight**: They are undereducated, overwhelmed, and unsupported.
* **Key Pain Points**: "I don't know where to start," "I don't trust banks," and "I know I should sort my money, but I don't act".

## Competitive Landscape
Mulo sits at the intersection of education, personal data, and comparison—a combination no existing player offers:

* **Monzo**: Banking app; no education layer.
* **Snoop**: Spending insights; no structured learning.
* **MoneySuperMarket**: Comparison site; sells products without educating.
* **MoneySavingExpert**: Editorial content; no app/data integration.
* **Duolingo**: Education mechanics inspiration; not in finance.
