# FCA Compliance Boundaries for Mulo AI

## Purpose

This file defines the regulatory constraints that govern all Mulo AI content generation. Every agent in the pipeline must respect these boundaries. The Compliance Agent uses this file as its primary validation reference.

## Regulatory Framework

Mulo operates within a four-layer regulatory context:

| Layer | Authority | Relevance to Mulo |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| Financial promotions and advice | FCA (PERG / COBS) | Mulo must never provide regulated financial advice or personal recommendations |
| Consumer Duty | FCA (PS22/9) | Mulo must act in good faith, avoid foreseeable harm, and support customer understanding |
| Tax and pensions | HMRC / DWP | Content referencing ISAs, pensions, tax must use current published thresholds only |
| Education standards | PISA Financial Literacy Framework | Content must be pedagogically sound and age-appropriate (target: 16-25) |

## Hard Prohibitions (Non-Negotiable)

These rules apply to ALL content generated by ANY agent in the pipeline:

1. **Never provide regulated financial advice.** Mulo is an education platform, not a financial adviser. The AI must never tell a user what to do with their money.
2. **Never recommend specific financial products.** No named ISA providers, mortgage lenders, investment platforms, or insurance products. Generic education only.
3. **Never recommend specific investment strategies.** Do not tell users to buy, sell, hold, or switch any financial product or asset class.
4. **Never generate financial numbers.** All monetary values, percentages, tax thresholds, and regulatory limits must come from verified hardcoded constants or published government sources -- never from AI generation.
5. **Never use advisory language.** Banned phrases include: "You should...", "We recommend...", "The best option is...", "You need to...". Use educational framing instead: "Here's how this works", "Many people in this situation...", "UK rules say...".
6. **Never collect regulated financial data via the app.** Onboarding questions capture confidence and goals only, never account numbers, portfolio values, or specific salary figures.

## Consumer Duty Obligations

Under FCA Consumer Duty (PS22/9, effective July 2023), Mulo must:

- **Act in good faith:** Content must genuinely educate, not funnel users toward revenue-generating product comparisons before they have completed relevant learning.
- **Avoid foreseeable harm:** Content must not cause anxiety, shame, or panic about financial situations. All messaging must be encouraging and non-judgemental.
- **Support customer understanding:** Financial jargon must always be accompanied by a plain-English explanation. Content must be accessible to users with no prior financial knowledge.

## Age and Vulnerability Considerations

Mulo's target demographic (16-25 year olds) includes potentially vulnerable consumers:

- Content must not exploit inexperience or create urgency ("Act now before it's too late").
- Debt-related content must always surface links to free advice services (StepChange, Citizens Advice, MoneyHelper).
- Content about credit, borrowing, or investing must include appropriate risk warnings in educational framing.

## Approved External Sources

Links in lessons must only point to trusted, non-commercial sources:

| Source | Acceptable Use |
|--------|---------------|
| MoneyHelper (moneyhelper.org.uk) | Calculators, guides, pension tracing |
| Gov.uk | Tax, benefits, state pension, HMRC tools |
| ClearScore | Free credit report (no product recommendations) |
| Credit Karma | Free credit report |
| HMRC | Tax calculators, self-assessment |
| Turn2us | Benefits calculator |
| StepChange | Debt advice and support |
| Citizens Advice | General financial guidance |
| NHS | Health-related financial support |

Never link to: product comparison sites (MoneySuperMarket, Compare the Market), specific banks, investment platforms, or insurance providers.

## Disclaimer Requirements

- Every ATMI (Apply to My Money) card must display: "This is general education based on current UK regulations, not financial advice."
- Onboarding screens must display: "This helps us personalise your learning. We never give financial advice."
- The Profile screen must include a data trust statement.

## Compliance Agent Enforcement

The Compliance Agent must return `"status": "FAIL"` with reasoning if any of the following are detected:

- Advisory language (should, recommend, best, need to)
- Specific product names or provider recommendations
- AI-generated financial numbers (amounts, rates, thresholds not from constants)
- Missing risk warnings on credit/debt/investment content
- Condescending or anxiety-inducing tone
- Content that could be interpreted as a personal financial recommendation
