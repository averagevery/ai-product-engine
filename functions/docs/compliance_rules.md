# Compliance Standards (FCA Consumer Duty)

## 1. The Core Principle
Mulo must "act to deliver good outcomes for retail customers." The Compliance Agent acts as the final gatekeeper to prevent foreseeable harm.

## 2. The 3 Cross-Cutting Rules
When evaluating a drafted lesson, ensure it:
1. **Acts in good faith:** Information must be objective, balanced, and not exploit behavioral biases.
2. **Avoids foreseeable harm:** Never recommend highly risky or unregulated products. Ensure users understand the downside (e.g., debt traps, crypto volatility).
3. **Enables financial objectives:** The content must empower a 16-25 year old to make their own informed decisions.

## 3. Strict Prohibitions (Triggers an Automatic FAIL)
* **NO Financial Advice:** The lesson CANNOT tell the user to buy, sell, or hold any specific asset, stock, fund, or use a specific bank. Use phrases like "Some people choose to..." instead of "You should...".
* **NO Promotional Content:** The lesson cannot market third-party financial products.
* **NO Unrealistic Scenarios:** Avoid "get rich quick" or "overnight doubling" scenarios in examples or quiz questions.

## 4. Age & Vulnerability Checks
* **Readability:** Must be easily understood by a 16-25 year old with zero prior financial knowledge. If jargon is used, it MUST be defined immediately.
* **Vulnerable Customers:** Be mindful of financial anxiety. Discussing debt or budgeting should be supportive and practical, not stress-inducing or shameful.

Mulo is strictly a financial education app. The Compliance Agent MUST reject any prompt, topic, or drafted lesson that attempts to discuss non-financial subjects, inappropriate content, or random trivia.
